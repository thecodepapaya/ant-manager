How it works

From what you did to what you can show

Your tools already know most of what you did. Ant Manager gathers it, makes sense of it, and keeps the receipts.

  1. Sources

    Git repos, GitHub, GitLab, calendars, tickets and your own notes.

  2. Evidence

    Every commit, review, meeting and note lands on your timeline, kept as it was.

  3. Narrative

    A model turns evidence into facts about what you did, each one citing its evidence.

  4. Reports and wins

    Daily to quarterly reports and a wins list for reviews, written from those facts.

Local first

Your data stays on this computer unless you choose a cloud model.

Always cited

Every line in a report links back to the work behind it.

Yours to correct

Edit or reject any fact. The model never overwrites your changes.

A closer look

Reflection, not surveillance

No screen recording, no keystroke logging, no productivity scores. Just the context you need to describe your work fairly.

Evidence, kept as it was

Commits, reviews, tickets, meetings and notes on one timeline. Star what matters, hide what doesn’t, and add context the tools couldn’t see, like “took 3 days because of flaky CI”.

Facts you can check

The Narrative is a list of typed facts: outcomes, problems solved, invisible work, hiccups, decisions and time spent. Each one says whether the model inferred it or you confirmed it, and cites the evidence it came from.

Confirm, edit or reject any fact. When new evidence touches a fact you changed, the model’s update waits for your review.

Reports that show their work

Daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly or any range, from templates like Standup, Manager update, Weekly review and Brag doc. Every line is footnoted to the facts behind it.

Reports aren’t edited by hand: correct a fact or add a note, then regenerate. Lock a report to keep it as it is; earlier versions are kept. Share it to Slack, email, Markdown or PDF.

Wins, ready for review season

Star evidence or accept an outcome and it lands in your wins log. The model also suggests wins for you to accept or reject. Filter by period and project, then use them in a report.

Notes from anywhere

Press ⌥⌘N on a Mac or Ctrl+Alt+N on Windows and Linux for a small note box over whatever you’re doing. Add a link, a project, or a screenshot of part of the screen: kind words in chat, a hallway decision, work no tool saw.

Set up in a few minutes

A short setup names your profile, finds which commit authors are you, picks the repos to watch, connects other sources and a model, then imports the last 30 days. Every step can be skipped.

After that it runs quietly from the menu bar or system tray, with an optional nudge at the end of the day to review what was drafted.

AI models

Bring your own model

Pick one model for everything. If you want, use a different one for reading screenshots, extracting facts or writing reports.

Every model must support structured output. Ant Manager checks this when you add one, so facts and reports always come back in a shape it can verify.

OpenRouter

One key for Claude, GPT, Gemini and open models, with prices shown when you pick one.

OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini

Use your own API key with the provider directly.

On this computer

Ollama and LM Studio are found automatically. Nothing leaves your machine.

On your network or a gateway

Any OpenAI-compatible or Ollama URL: a GPU box on your LAN, vLLM, llama.cpp, LiteLLM or a company gateway.

Privacy

Where your data goes, plainly

There are no Ant Manager servers, no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry. Nothing is sent to the project or its authors.

Kept on your computer

Evidence, facts and reports live in a local database per profile. API keys and tokens go in your system’s secret store, never in the database or backups.

The database isn’t encrypted; it relies on your account and disk encryption.

Sent to the model you choose

A model on this computer keeps everything here. A cloud model, or one on another machine, receives excerpts of your evidence: diffs, PR and issue text, meeting details, transcripts, notes and screenshots. You’re asked once per address before anything is sent.

Fetched from your sources

Connected services see your token and the requests for your activity. Backups go to a folder you pick. They aren’t encrypted, so a synced folder gives that service a copy.

Download

Get Ant Manager

Free, Apache 2.0 licensed, and built from the public source by GitHub Actions. Every release is on GitHub. If the Releases page is still empty, the first release isn’t out yet.

macOS

macOS 12 or later. Apple silicon and Intel in one .dmg.

Windows

Windows 10 or later, 64-bit. A setup .exe.

Linux

64-bit AppImage for Ubuntu 22.04 or similar. Needs libsecret (GNOME Keyring or KWallet).

Download from GitHub Releases

Builds aren’t signed yet, so your system will warn you the first time. This is expected until code-signing certificates are in place.

macOS: open the app once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway. On macOS 14 and earlier, Control-click the app and choose Open. Windows: on the SmartScreen prompt, choose More info, then Run anyway. Linux: make the AppImage executable (chmod +x) and run it.

You also need git installed; the app helps if it’s missing. There’s no auto-update yet, so check the Releases page for new versions.
